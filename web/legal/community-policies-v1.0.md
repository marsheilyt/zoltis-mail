# Zoltis Mail Community Edition policies

Version 1.0 · 18 November 2026 · Zealartis Global Technologies, Wyoming, USA

Before you create email accounts on this server, read and accept these policies. They cover the free Community Edition and its license key. The full Terms of Service are at https://zoltismail.com/legal/terms.

## 1. You are the operator

- This server runs on your own hardware or VPS. You, not Zealartis, are the operator of the email service for everyone you create an account for.
- You are responsible for securing and updating the server, backing up its data, and handling abuse complaints about mail it sends.
- Zealartis never receives the mail, addresses or files stored on this server.
- Give the people you create accounts for your own rules for using email (an internal email policy), and tell them who to contact for help.

## 2. Community limits

- Up to 10 email accounts, 2 domains and 5 aliases (postmaster@ does not count), and 720 MB per mailbox, attachments included.
- One server per license key. Don't open several Zoltis accounts or keys for one organisation to get around these limits.
- Need more? Move to a managed Mail Pro plan from Admin console → License.

## 3. License key

- Every server needs a free license key. Add it within 7 days of installing: get it at zoltismail.com (My account → License keys → Community Edition) and paste it in Admin console → License. After 7 days without a key, mail keeps working, but new accounts and domains are paused until you add one.
- A key works on one server only: the server IP address you enter when you get it, and only the domains named in it, which must be domains you own or control.
- A key is valid for one year from activation. Renew it free in the same zoltismail.com account, from 30 days before it ends; the key stays the same. If you don't renew, mail keeps working, but new accounts and domains are paused until you do.
- Once a day the server sends the licensing service only: its license ID, install ID, a random ID for the running copy, edition, version, hostname and IP address, and whether its program files are the unchanged official release (with the names of any changed files). Never email, addresses or usage data.
- Zealartis may revoke a key obtained with false information or used for abuse.

## 4. Acceptable use

You and every account holder on this server must not use it to:

- send spam, bulk mail to people who didn't agree to receive it, or mail to bought, rented or scraped lists
- send commercial email that breaks the CAN-SPAM Act or other anti-spam laws
- send phishing, spoofed or deceptive messages, malware or links to malware
- store or send child sexual abuse material, content that infringes others' rights, threats or harassment, or anything else illegal
- run an open relay, attack or probe other systems, or access accounts without permission
- remove, bypass or tamper with the license check, the edition limits or the suspension behaviour
